Our story

Cinci Beauty Spa is a beauty, skin and wellness spa on Pretorius Street in Pretoria Central. We offer massage, facials, deep cleansing, dermaplaning, back scrubs, wood therapy, waxing, pedicures, nails and lashes. Add your own story
